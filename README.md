# Smart Studio Web App 📸🏛️
### Luxury Photography & Videography Client Portal & Studio Management
**4 Kabama Estate Layout, Abuja, Nigeria**

Smart Studio Web App is an independent, state-of-the-art client portal and studio operations web application engineered for Smart Studio Abuja.

---

## ✨ Features

- **👑 VIP Client Portal & 3D Holographic Card**:
  - Interactive 3D metal VIP membership card with gyro tilt and gloss effects.
  - Profile image customization with presets and direct image upload.
  - Ambient Studio Lounge soundscape player with frequency wave animations.
  - Live production countdown timer to next scheduled shoot milestone.

- **📅 Online Session Builder & Customizer**:
  - 4-step booking wizard with package selection, luxury add-ons, wardrobe notes, and calendar date picker.
  - Real-time price calculator in Nigerian Naira (₦).

- **💳 Multi-Method Payment & Deposit Engine**:
  - **Full Payment vs 50% VIP Retainer Deposit** selection.
  - **Paystack Debit Card & Online Checkout** (Visa, Mastercard, Verve, USSD).
  - **Direct Bank Wire / Instant Transfer** (Guaranty Trust Bank corporate details with 1-click clipboard copy).
  - **Studio Reception POS / Cash on Arrival** voucher.
  - **Cryptocurrency (USDT TRC20)** for international & Web3 clients.
  - Instant digital VAT invoice & official receipt with print format.

- **⭐ Verified Client Reviews & Reputation**:
  - Category filters (*Portraits*, *Weddings*, *Corporate*, *Creative*).
  - Interactive 5-star rating picker with highlight tag chips.
  - Official Studio Director response badges and in-card reply tool.

- **🖼️ Digital Photo Proofing Suite**:
  - Interactive Before/After Master Retouch comparison slider.
  - Client favorite selection counter with quota tracking.
  - Frame-by-frame retouching note requests & full-screen lightbox.

- **Director & Studio Manager Console**:
  - Advance shoot production milestones (`Booked` &rarr; `Shooting` &rarr; `Proofing` &rarr; `Retouching` &rarr; `Completed`).
  - Revenue analytics and booking management.
