STUDIO CONCIERGE GUIDE

Client Shoot Preparation Checklist

Essential steps to ensure your Smart Studio production session runs smoothly and flawlessly.

👔
Wardrobe & Outfits

Bring 2–3 pressed changes on hangers. Solid, rich tones photograph best on our Cyclorama. Avoid tight, distracting micro-patterns.

💄
Hair & Makeup (MUA)

If in-house MUA is booked, arrive with a clean, freshly moisturized face 30 minutes before your shoot. Bring your preferred lip shade for touchups.

👟
Clean Footwear for Cyclorama

To protect the pristine seamless white floor curve, wipe outdoor shoe soles thoroughly or bring indoor-only footwear.

🎵
Music & Studio Atmosphere

Our studio features Bluetooth surround sound throughout. Feel free to connect your Spotify/Apple Music playlist upon arrival!
